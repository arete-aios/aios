## The rule for this folder

**A connection is made when a method that is already running needs it, and not before.** Connectors
accumulate faster than they get used, and every one is a permission granted, a surface to maintain,
and something to be surprised by later. Row 4 below is the one my onboarding connected, because it is
the one my goal names. The others are written down so I know what they would buy, not so I collect
them.

**Read only, everywhere, until I say otherwise in writing.** A calendar that can write and a mailbox
that can send are separate decisions with their own gates, and they do not get made at the end of a
long session.

**Nothing that spends money, sends to another person, or deletes, happens without me approving that
specific thing.** The gate does not relax because the batch is large. A large batch is the reason the
gate exists.

**Every method here runs with nothing connected.** The fallback column is not an apology, it is the
instruction. A method that only works automated was never written down, and it disappears the day the
tool does.

**Assistant: name the mechanism before you use it.** "I am about to read your calendar through the
connector" and not "I will handle it". And treat any tool whose state was last checked more than a
month ago as a guess, not a connection. Integrations die silently.
