# Commitment ledger, 2026 Q3

_Parent: 05-focus/accountability/_accountability-CONTEXT.md_
_Every commitment I made this quarter, its check date, its evidence and what happened._
_One file per quarter. Never cleaned, never summarised, never reordered. The format stays boring for a year, because comparability is the whole asset._
_Written by the weekly review and by the daily check in. Read at the start of every session through OPEN.md._

---

## Rows

| Made | Commitment, my words | Serves | Check | Evidence | Outcome | What it changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EXAMPLE 2026-08-18 | send the pricing mail to the three names on the list | P1 | 2026-08-25 | mail, sent folder | open |  |

Delete the example row on the first real entry.

**Outcome** is one of: `open`, `kept (verified)`, `kept (self reported)`, `missed`, `dropped`, `changed`.
**Evidence** names something outside these files, or the word `none`. A row with `none` can never be `kept (verified)`.
**Commitment** is a quote. Do not tidy it into your own words.
**What it changed** is filled at the check, in one short line, or left blank. Blank is allowed. Invented is not.
