# How COMMS7 chooses the next action

Parent: [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md).

Ask: Is it actually addressed to the owner? What is unresolved? Is it already
resolved in newer accessible context? Why now, given deadlines, consequences,
North Star and Focus? Which next step does the evidence support?

“Thanks” without a new request usually needs no task. “I'll send the slides tomorrow”
keeps delivery open after the acknowledgement is sent. “I'll answer on Friday” is
Waiting until the agreed point, not an invented earlier chase. A relevant newsletter
may be reading material without becoming a reply task.

Do not mistake message volume, unread state, enthusiasm or a connection badge for
importance. A real low-strategy-fit obligation still needs an honest decision;
strategic direction is a prioritisation lens, not an excuse to ignore commitments.
