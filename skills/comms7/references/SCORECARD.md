# Illustrative planning estimates

Parent: [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md).

Business-fit, complexity and usage figures in the human guide are illustrative
planning estimates, not measured performance or proven savings. The profile is
an advisory business handling customer communication, decisions and partner follow-up.

Fit is higher for operations, leadership and sales because these repeatedly depend
on commitments and follow-up. Complexity rises from a few supplied conversations,
to two connected channels, to broader coverage with identity, state and privacy checks.
Token ranges are workload assumptions; the offline helper reports characters and
selection counts, not provider-token telemetry. Actual cost must be measured on a
specific model, source volume and run. The separate AIOS integration label is a
qualitative dependency assessment and never a quality or accuracy score.
