**3. Follow up on the proposal — Gmail.** A partner promised a reply on 20 October; no newer reply is supplied. The example decision is to prepare a short follow-up. Draft: “Hello, following up on our proposal. Is this still relevant? If so, let's agree the next step.” This is unsent.

**5. Waiting for the partner's file — previous open item.** The partner promised material by 22 October. Decision: WAITING, check 23 October. It persists even when no new message arrives. In this example its decision was accepted earlier in the same review.

## What progress means

Three of five items have explicit decisions: DOING, REPLY and WAITING. Decision progress is 60%; two need the owner's choice. This does not mean three external obligations are complete.
