08 / Skills · COMMS7

GitHub · comms7 ↗
← All skills

Who needs a reply, and what you are waiting for

COMMS7 is a written method, not an app. Every morning it reads Nora's chosen channels, sorts each conversation by who has to act next, drafts the replies she asks for and checks a short watch list of posts for comments worth answering. The AI reads and drafts; Nora decides and sends.

Open COMMS7 on GitHub

When it runs

Every morning, with a full review on Mondays

Inside the Maker's 08:05 morning run, or whenever Nora types COMMS7.

Who acts

You decide · AI reads and drafts · code collects

In this example, “you” is Nora.

What it never does

Send, post or delete

A draft stays a draft until Nora sends it. Nothing is accepted or promised in her name.

Goal

Know who needs a reply, what you need to do and what you are waiting for, with one clear next step for each item and no open promise lost.

How one review runs

Seven steps from the request to the saved decisions. Two of them are Nora's, one is plain code, and the AI does the reading, sorting and drafting in between.

The same seven steps in words

  1. 01Ask for COMMS7You · chat or the 08:05 runType COMMS7 and say what to look at: everything, only replies, or one conversation. On weekdays the Maker's morning run asks for her; on Mondays it is the full review.
  2. 02Set the scopeAI · reads the settingsThe AI takes the sources, how far back to read and the watch list from _comms-CONTEXT.md, plus the open items from the last review. It asks only what would change today's review.
  3. 03Collect the messagesCode · five channelsThe connections fetch the last 14 days (the guild chat 7) and new comments on watched posts. A small script removes duplicates and marks what is new since the last review; it does not judge what a message means.
  4. 04Read and sortAI · whole threadsThe AI reads each whole thread, including what Nora already sent, and gives it one state: reply needed, waiting on them, for information, or closed. Watched posts are checked for questions.
  5. 05Show the few that matterAI · drafts, not sentUp to five items, real deadlines first, then the north star and this week's projects. Each has a reason, one next step and its source; the replies Nora asked for are drafted and left unsent.
  6. 06Decide and sendYou · item by itemFor each item Nora replies, does it, waits, moves it to later or skips it. She sends and posts herself; the AI updates that item and shows the next one.
  7. 07Save and closeAI · project filesEach decision goes into the project file the conversation belongs to, and anything waiting gets a date to check. The AI reads the files back and closes with EXIT7, so tomorrow starts from here.

Principles

What the method holds to, every run.

Who acts next
Every conversation gets exactly one state: reply needed, waiting on them, for information, or closed.
Deadlines first
Real deadlines and consequences come first, then the north star and this week's projects; unread counts and loud messages do not decide, and no due date is invented.
Open promises stay open
“I'll send the slides tomorrow” is still open after the thank-you goes out; silence or archiving does not close it.
A draft is not a reply
Nothing counts as answered until Nora sends it, and a suggested comment is only a draft until she posts it or skips it.
Say what was not read
If a channel could not be read, the review says so instead of guessing what was in it.

When buyers ask an AI, is Vale Studio named?

GEO7 is a public method for being found by the right buyers, in search and in AI answers. Nora uses it for one narrow question: do AI assistants recommend Vale Studio when a buyer asks? Once a month the same answers are collected and scored the same way, and the gaps become a short, ordered list of fixes for her website.

Open GEO7 on GitHub · the public method and its helper scripts

When it runs

1st of each month

Last run 1 Nov · next 1 Dec. Three answers arrive by API, two are pasted by Nora.

Who acts

  • AIasks the assistants and scores the answers
  • coderepeats the same checks, the same way, every month

What it never does

Goal

A buyer who asks an AI assistant one of the five questions hears Vale Studio named, and that answer leads to a call with Nora.

How one monthly round runs

Six stages from the public method, applied to Nora's five questions. Code repeats the checks, AI selects and explains, Nora decides. The saved files are what each round leaves behind.

    Stage: audit

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

  5. 5

    Approve and buildYou

    Stage: implementation

    Nora approves each change, and the website project builds it when it restarts on 1 Dec. GEO7 itself publishes nothing.

  6. 6

    Ask and scoreAI + code

    Stage: measurement

    On the 1st, the same five questions go to the same five assistants: three by API, two pasted by Nora. AI marks each answer placed, named or not; ai_visibility.py repeats the count and leaves errors out.

Principles

Five rules that keep one month comparable with the next.

Same pairs, month to month
Months are compared only on the same question and assistant pairs. A new question or assistant starts its own series.
A place needs a numbered list
A place such as #2 is recorded only when the answer is a numbered list. Otherwise a recommendation counts as named, without a place.
An error is never a zero
A failed or empty answer is left out and noted. That is why November reads 4 of 24, not 4 of 25.
Blockers before volume
If a page cannot be read, or answers a different question, fix that before writing anything new.
Buyer intent over broad topics
One narrow question a real buyer asks is worth more than a broad topic that many people search for.

P1–P3 is an order of work. It is judged from the buyer's path, the strength of the evidence and whether the fix can be checked. It is not a measured effect on AI answers; when the evidence is thin, the first task is a check.
