# CRM7 · a relationship workspace

Parent: [CC7 Command Center](command-center.md). Portable workspace recipe,
2026-11-13. This does not install a CRM backend or import anybody's contacts.

Use for one professional relationship, meeting preparation or a follow-up view.
Choose the owner's existing CRM/contact records as authority. A person mentioned
in a document is not automatically a participant, prospect or permission to
contact them. Resolve ambiguous names before linking records.

Keep three views: overview, linked projects/tasks, and dated meetings/outcomes.
For each factual claim or commitment retain its source and actor. Separate what
was said from an AI suggestion; anchor “tomorrow” to the meeting date. Give an
open promise an owner, a real due date if known, and one useful next step.

Before a meeting, show the latest relevant facts, previous decisions, unresolved
questions and commitments. After it, preserve the original, link the accepted
notes and update only authorised fields. Read back any write. Reuse stable IDs
and the existing task writer; do not create a second contacts or tasks store.

Relationship assessments and personal notes stay in the owner's private view.
Do not invent a relationship score, sales stage, message or meeting. A draft is
not sent and a suggested follow-up is not an overdue obligation. No contact,
calendar, account or message change happens just from building this view.

Check with a fictional fixture first: one known person, one unresolved identity,
and one promise with an unknown date. Unknown must stay unknown. The output is
one useful relationship view with verified links and a clear next action.
