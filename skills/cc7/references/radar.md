# RADAR · an opportunity watch

Parent: [CC7 Command Center](command-center.md). Portable workspace recipe,
2026-11-17. This does not install a job board, scraper or alert service, and it
does not apply for anything.

Use when the owner wants their Command Center to keep an eye on opportunities:
paid work or projects (for example fractional or freelance), partnerships,
speaking slots, and communities or events worth joining. Start by asking for
the owner's criteria and sources; an empty criteria file means no list yet.

Sources are only the ones the owner names or has already authorised: alert
e-mails, saved searches, newsletters, call-for-papers lists, groups they belong
to. Respect each site's terms; do not scrape what forbids it, bypass logins or
bot protection. A new source, alert or account is the owner's decision.

Keep one weekly shortlist of up to five opportunities. A shorter or empty list
is useful when fewer qualify. Use checked sources or details the owner pastes.
For each find record its source or link, when it was found and checked, the
stream, fit against the agreed criteria, a short reason and one thing to verify
before acting. Mark a missing rate, client, location or other field as unknown.
Keep entries with unconfirmed mandatory criteria as leads to verify. Drop stale
posts and duplicates from the shortlist.

The owner sets the statuses: new, worth a look, applied/waiting, declined, won,
not for me. Keep their dates. “Not for me” marks sharpen the next week's
criteria. A short four-week review per stream (found, worth a look, applied,
result) shows which stream actually works.

An accepted opportunity becomes a project card, a CRM person or a task in the
existing writers. The RADAR list is a shortlist, not a second task or contacts
database. Drafting an application or message happens only on request, and a
draft is not sent.

Run it when the owner asks or as a weekly manual review. A schedule, new
connector or paid search needs the owner's specific authorisation. Never apply,
send, register, join, create alerts or change profiles on their behalf.

## Try a first review

Use these three fictional notices as if the owner pasted them on 25 November
2026. The labels A, B and C are the source references for the exercise.

Agree the rubric before ranking. This owner takes engagements that confirm at
least €500 and run remotely or in Riga from 1 to 31 December. Give 50 points
for a small-business audience, 30 for a practical AI operations topic and 20
for a hands-on workshop. Shortlist candidates who meet both mandatory
requirements and score at least 70 out of 100.

| Source | Details from the notice |
| --- | --- |
| A · Riga Small Business Lab | Riga, 9 December, €650. A hands-on workshop on AI for small-business operations. |
| B · Founder Practice Circle | Remote, 15 December. A hands-on workshop on AI for small-business operations. Fee omitted. |
| C · Community Learning Day | Riga, 19 December, €600. A panel on local history for the general public. |

**Expected shortlist:** A only, 100/100, based on the pasted notice. Before
acting, check that the 9 December slot is still open. Mark B's fee as unknown
and ask the owner whether to verify it. C clears the fee and timing requirements
but scores 0/100 on the agreed fit criteria. One qualified item is enough.
