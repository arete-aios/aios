# The thirty second pitch: seven blocks

Loaded when the owner is building or revising what they say. Part of [WARM7](../SKILL.md).

The test is not whether it sounds good. The test is whether a listener can **repeat it to someone else**. A pitch that cannot be repeated cannot be referred, and referral is the whole point of a warm list.

## 1 · Unique selling proposition

What only this owner brings. A method, a tool, an unusual combination of experience.

Ask: what do you do that others do not, and what would a competent competitor be unable to copy by Friday?

> *"Unlike traditional coaching, my approach combines cognitive behavioural techniques with personalised wellness plans."*

## 2 · Target audience

Who benefits most. Demographics, but more usefully the situation they are in.

Resist "everyone". A pitch aimed at everyone is unrepeatable, because the listener cannot picture a specific person to send.

> *"I help high achieving professionals."*

## 3 · Pain point

The problem the audience already knows they have. Not the problem the owner finds interesting.

Include why it matters now. A problem with no urgency produces agreement and no action.

> *"Many professionals struggle to keep any balance while scaling a business."*

## 4 · No-brainer offer

More than a discount. A way in that is easy to say yes to because the risk is gone.

Three ways to build one:

**Remove the risk.** A guarantee, money back, or nothing owed unless it works.
**Add value.** Something extra that costs the owner little and is worth a lot to the receiver.
**Make it easy to start.** A free audit, a consultation, a short trial. Let them see it before deciding.

Eight ready openers built on this are in [outreach-approaches.md](outreach-approaches.md).

## 5 · Specific outcome

What is measurably different afterwards, and roughly when.

Numbers if there are honest ones. A time frame always, because "eventually" reads as "never".

> *"A healthier balance between personal and professional life, inside three months."*

## 6 · The service

What is actually delivered, briefly. What happens, in what order.

This is the block people put first and it belongs sixth. Nobody cares what it is until they recognise the problem.

> *"Personalised coaching programmes that integrate mindfulness practice."*

## 7 · Benefit to the client

Why choose this over the alternative, including the alternative of doing nothing. What changes for them personally, not just commercially.

> *"You reduce stress and increase output, and get a life that is actually yours."*

## How to test it

Write the thirty second version. **Memorise it**, because reading it aloud is a different thing entirely and the difference shows.

**Say it to ten or more people.** Then ask exactly two questions:

1. **What did you understand?**
2. **What was unclear?**

Not "what do you think", which produces encouragement, and not "did you like it", which produces politeness. These two produce information.

**Revise. Go around again**, with the same people or new ones. Two rounds is usually enough for something sayable. One round is never enough.

**What a passing pitch looks like:** the listener repeats it back roughly right, in their own words, without being prompted. If they can do that, they can also say it to someone else when the owner is not in the room. That is the whole objective.
