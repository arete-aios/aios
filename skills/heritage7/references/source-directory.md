# HERITAGE7 source directory

Links and access notes checked on 25 November 2026. Use the section that matches the place, year and type of event. An index or finding aid can identify a promising record. Cite the actual document when you can see it and label the lead when you can only see the index.

### WWI and the Latvian War of Independence

Start with the Latvian War Museum's riflemen names when the lead predates Latvian statehood. For the Latvian War of Independence (1918–1920), search `Latvijas armija`, `Neatkarības karš`, `Brīvības cīņas`, regiment name, `pavēles` (orders), `personālsastāvs` (personnel) and, when relevant, `Lāčplēša Kara ordenis`. Use the [museum's interactive map](https://www.karamuzejs.lv/node/481) for unit movements and place context. Use [LNA’s research route](https://www.arhivi.gov.lv/lv/ieteikumi-dzimtas-petniekiem) to locate personnel files, regiment orders or award files. A battle's location or a regiment's route alone establishes no individual’s presence.

## Soviet WWII records

| Source | What to look for | Access and what to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ОБД Мемориал](https://obd-memorial.ru/html/) | Soviet WWII casualty, missing, hospital, captivity and burial records. Try Cyrillic surname and given name, patronymic, birth year, place, and recruitment office (`военкомат`). | Public search form opened during checking. Open the underlying image where available. Record archive, fond, inventory, file and image or sheet, with the original Cyrillic. |
| [Память народа](https://pamyat-naroda.ru/heroes/) | Individual service entries, military units, operations and award links. Search by Cyrillic name and unit and compare dates. | Official endpoint. Automated reading returned HTTP 401 on the check date. Record access as unavailable if it fails and use other institutions or lawfully held copies. |
| [Подвиг народа](https://podvignaroda.ru/) | Soviet military decorations and recommendation sheets. Look for award type, award order, unit and date. A recommendation describes what was submitted. The order can establish what was granted. | Official endpoint timed out on the check date. Record access as unavailable if it fails. Compare the archival citation with the same document if surfaced elsewhere. |

Prefer public read-only access for these Russian sites. Keep account credentials, identity documents and living relatives’ data out of their searches. A scan indexed by several sites remains one source. When an endpoint cannot be reached, try LNA and the Latvian War Museum for Latvian holdings and the [Bundesarchiv POW guide](https://www.bundesarchiv.de/im-archiv-recherchieren/archivgut-recherchieren/nach-themen/kriegsgefangenschaft-im-zweiten-weltkrieg/), DRK or Arolsen for captivity, persecution and displacement. Choose the institution by event and record type. Access failure leaves the person's record status unknown.

## What to do when a catalogue gives only a reference

1. Save the catalogue URL and the exact signature, including the collection, inventory, file and page if available. Record the person and event anchors that distinguish this candidate, plus the state of access.
2. Prepare a request for one fact or one identified file. Include name variants, approximate period, place, regiment or parish, known identifiers and the page or file you want. Ask whether a scan can be supplied, whether fees apply and what permission is needed to include an image in a family edition. The requester signs in, provides required identification and submits the request.
3. In the family book, cite every image and name its rights or permission. When reuse is uncertain, use an attributed citation and a short reading or summary. Keep private certificates and details about living people in the private research archive unless the people concerned have agreed to circulation.
