# AIOS Personal Audit — how the owner appears on the public web

Everything below is addressed to the assistant. Run it when the owner says **AIOS Personal Audit**, asks for a personal public-footprint audit, or asks how they appear online.

This is not `AUDIT7`. `AUDIT7` checks the AIOS system. Personal Audit checks the public picture of the owner and compares it with what they say they are building.

The command itself is permission to inspect public web pages about the owner. It is not permission to access private accounts, paid data brokers, leaked data, login-gated pages, or information about family members.

## First message

Before searching, tell the owner plainly:

> I will now search the public web for your name and known professional profiles, open the pages that appear to belong to you, and build a sourced picture of how a stranger would understand you. I will separate facts, other people's words, and my interpretation. Nothing will be saved into your AIOS until you approve it.

If more than one person has the same name and the constitution does not provide enough to distinguish them, ask one short identity question. Otherwise begin without another permission step.

If web access is unavailable, say that the audit cannot be run yet. Do not turn a pasted biography into a fake web audit.

## Search in passes

1. **Identity pass:** full name with location, current organization, role, and known domains. Exclude namesakes before interpreting anything.
2. **Owned pass:** personal website, company pages, author pages, newsletters, portfolios, repositories, and profiles the owner controls.
3. **Earned pass:** event pages, podcasts, interviews, media, partner pages, awards, reviews, and credible third-party mentions.
4. **Social pass:** public professional profiles and the recent themes visible there. Do not enter private groups or authenticated accounts.
5. **Consistency pass:** compare names, role, biography, offer, proof, dates, links, and calls to action across surfaces.

Prefer primary pages and current sources. Record the page title, URL, relevant fact, and access date for every claim used.

## What the report contains

1. **What a stranger sees first** — the likely public one-sentence impression.
2. **Confirmed public facts** — only facts supported by cited pages.
3. **How others describe the owner** — attributable quotes or faithful paraphrases, never invented sentiment.
4. **The visible expertise** — repeated topics, evidence, results, and associations.
5. **Gaps and contradictions** — stale roles, broken links, inconsistent bios, unsupported claims, or important work that is invisible.
6. **Search and identity risks** — namesake confusion, old pages outranking current ones, exposed information, or unclear ownership.
7. **The difference from the intended direction** — compare the public picture with `CONSTITUTION.md` and the current business profile.
8. **Five actions** — ordered by impact and effort, with one action small enough to complete today.
9. **Sources** — direct links next to the claims they support.

Do not reproduce home addresses, personal phone numbers, private email addresses, family details, or unrelated sensitive information even if a public page exposes them. Name the exposure as a risk without repeating the value.

## Save only after review

Show the report in chat first. Ask the owner which findings are wrong, belong to a namesake, or should not enter durable memory.

Suggested destination after approval: `02-memory/public-footprint/personal-audit-YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Update the owning CONTEXT file. Promote a fact into the constitution or business profile only with separate approval.

## Definition of done

- The identity match is explicit and namesakes are excluded.
- Every important public claim has a direct source.
- Other people's words are separated from the assistant's interpretation.
- Sensitive exposed values are not repeated.
- The report compares public reality with the owner's intended direction.
- The owner reviewed it before anything was saved.
