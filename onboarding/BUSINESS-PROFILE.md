# AIOS Business Profile — seven blocks and one clear sentence

Everything below is addressed to the assistant. Run it when the owner says **AIOS Business Profile**, asks to create or update their business profile, or needs the system to understand one business well enough to plan from facts.

One business or substantial project gets one profile. Never combine several businesses in one file merely because one person owns them.

## Start from evidence

Read the constitution and relevant memory first. Use attached material before asking the owner to remember it again: CV or biography, LinkedIn, website, offers, proposals, testimonials, call transcripts, numbers, and previous decisions.

If key sources are missing, ask once for the CV, LinkedIn, website, and permission to search the business or owner online. Do not block the profile if they choose not to provide them. Mark the gap `NOT CONFIRMED`.

Anything the owner said is confirmed. Anything found online remains unconfirmed until the owner confirms it. Date facts that can change, including price, audience size, revenue, team size, and current channel performance.

## The seven blocks

Build the draft in exactly this order:

1. **Identity and business in plain language** — what it is, where it operates, and the owner's real role.
2. **Direction, principles, values, and boundaries** — what it is trying to become and what it will not do.
3. **Main goal and measure** — one primary outcome, the number that proves it, and the time horizon if known.
4. **One target customer** — their situation, pain, desired change, buying trigger, and the words they use themselves.
5. **Offer and delivery** — what is sold, how it is delivered, the promised result, the price if confirmed, and what is explicitly not included.
6. **Evidence and assets** — experience, client results, testimonials, examples, content, audience, relationships, tools, data, and reusable work already built.
7. **The next 30 days** — one channel, one concrete next step, one measure, and one stop-or-change rule.

Show each block as:

- `🟢` confirmed and sufficient to act on;
- `🟡` partly known, with the exact missing fact;
- `🔴` too uncertain to plan from.

Give an overall completeness percentage, but never let the percentage hide the real gap. One red target-customer block matters more than five polished paragraphs elsewhere.

Ask no more than five questions in one batch. Prefer a draft the owner can correct over a blank form.

## The super sentence

After the seven blocks are approved, write five one-sentence versions using this shape:

> who I am · who I help · which problem · which result · how

Each version must be understandable in about three seconds and grounded in confirmed facts. Avoid category jargon, vague transformation language, and claims the evidence cannot support.

Recommend:

- one **clear version** that the target customer will understand immediately;
- one **bolder version** that carries more of the owner's distinctive point of view without becoming unprovable.

The owner chooses or rewrites the final sentence. Put it at the top of the profile and link it from the relevant memory CONTEXT file.

## Save and maintain it

Suggested destination: `02-memory/businesses/<business-slug>/business-profile.md`. Use the existing memory structure if one already exists; do not create a parallel tree.

Before saving, show the complete draft and ask for approval. After saving:

1. update the owning CONTEXT file;
2. show any constitution line that should change, but do not change the constitution without a separate approval;
3. add unresolved items to the profile's `Open questions` section;
4. tell the owner the one weakest block and the next question that would improve it most.

## Definition of done

- One business, not an accidental mixture.
- Seven blocks, each with an honest status.
- The target customer and offer use evidence, not generic marketing language.
- The 30-day move has a measure and stop-or-change rule.
- The owner approved the profile and chose the final one-sentence introduction.
