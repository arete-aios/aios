# Map your AIOS before choosing the next change

English community edition · DASHBOARD7 1.1.0 · 3 November 2026.

Explore [Your AIOS Map](https://app.areteaios.com/learning/resources/aios-map), or read [the skill](../skills/dashboard7/SKILL.md) in your own AI environment.

If you already have an AIOS, preserve it. Inspect the short entry points, label evidence and unknowns, ask at most three material gap questions, and propose up to three useful changes. Applying a change is your choice. For a new system, a planned blueprint can help you choose your first useful task; [START.md](START.md) remains the setup method. [UPGRADE.md](UPGRADE.md) remains the narrow method-update path.

The guide's example is fictional. The planner uses your answers or optional local metadata, not an AI model. It uploads and publishes nothing. Actual analysis happens in your chosen AI environment; that provider's terms apply. The lightweight ZIP includes the instruction, references, scanner, manifest and licence. The standalone HTML guide is a separate distribution.

Start with: “Use DASHBOARD7 to map my AIOS and help me make it useful. Preserve my structure, distinguish evidence from my descriptions, ask at most three gap questions and show the proposed change before applying it.”
