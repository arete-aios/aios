# AIOS Deep Dive — understand the owner before advising them

Everything below is addressed to the assistant. Run it when the owner says **AIOS Deep Dive**, asks for self-reflection questions, wants a self-coaching conversation, or asks for help understanding their larger potential.

The goal is not a long personality report. The goal is a confirmed map that changes advice: what brings the owner alive, what they are unusually able to do, what keeps repeating, what is in the way, and which future is actually theirs.

## Before the first question

1. Read `CONSTITUTION.md`, the relevant memory CONTEXT files, and any previous deep-dive file.
2. Say what you already believe to be true in no more than five lines and ask the owner to correct it.
3. Tell them the short pass takes about 25 minutes. They may skip any question and stop at any time.
4. Do not diagnose mental illness, trauma, or personality disorders. Name observed patterns as patterns, not identities.

If the owner has already given an answer in a file, do not ask it again. Offer the existing answer and ask whether it is still true.

## Start with a brain dump

Invite the owner to speak or write freely for up to ten minutes:

> What is taking up space in your head right now? Include what you want, what is not working, what you are avoiding, and what you keep returning to. Do not organise it.

Save the raw words before interpreting them. A useful default is `02-memory/deep-dive/raw-YYYY-MM-DD.md`, unless the memory structure already has a better home.

Then separate what arrived into five provisional groups: **desire, evidence, pattern, constraint, open question**. Show the groups briefly and ask what you misunderstood.

## The interview

Ask one question at a time. Choose the next question from the answer you just received; do not display the whole questionnaire.

Cover these seven territories, but stop when the map is already useful:

1. **Aliveness.** When in the last two years did the owner feel most awake, absorbed, proud, or in flow? What exactly were they doing?
2. **Evidence of strength.** Which outcomes have repeated across different jobs or projects? Ask for numbers, time spans, and what changed because of them.
3. **Recognition.** What do capable people repeatedly ask this owner for help with? What would a former colleague recommend them for in thirty seconds?
4. **Values and boundaries.** What must be true for work to feel like theirs? What success would they refuse because of the cost?
5. **Recurring patterns.** What do they start, avoid, overbuild, abandon, or return to? Name contradictions without trying to solve them immediately.
6. **Reality.** What time, money, energy, relationships, tools, reputation, and obligations shape the next twelve months? Ask permission before exact financial or health questions; summaries are valid.
7. **Future potential.** If the next three years used their strongest evidence rather than their loudest fear, what might become possible? What would make that future visibly real?

Close with two questions:

- What did I not ask that I should know?
- What do you most need your AIOS to help you do now?

## The output

Write a draft with these sections:

1. **The owner's sentence** — their current description of who they are becoming.
2. **Where energy appears** — specific situations, not adjectives.
3. **Proven strengths** — evidence and outcomes.
4. **Values and non-negotiables.**
5. **Patterns that help and patterns that interfere.**
6. **Resources and constraints** — date anything that can become stale.
7. **The larger potential** — one grounded possibility, not a motivational slogan.
8. **Open questions** — anything still uncertain or found rather than confirmed.
9. **One next experiment** — small enough to produce evidence within 30 days.

Mark every inference as an inference. Show the whole draft and ask what is wrong or missing. Save only after the owner approves it.

Default destination: `02-memory/deep-dive/deep-dive-YYYY-MM-DD.md`. If that would create a second structure beside an existing personal profile or inventory, use the existing node and update its CONTEXT file.

Do not rewrite `CONSTITUTION.md` automatically. After the deep dive, separately show the two or three lines that deserve promotion into the constitution and ask for approval.

## Definition of done

- At least one piece of advice changed because of a real answer.
- Strengths are supported by evidence, not praise.
- Stale facts are dated and uncertain facts are marked.
- The owner recognizes themselves in the draft and has corrected it.
- The next experiment is connected to the potential described above.

For the deeper inventory of assets, runway, audience, tools, and obstacles, continue with [`../skills/deepdive7/SKILL.md`](../skills/deepdive7/SKILL.md). Do not repeat answers already captured here.
