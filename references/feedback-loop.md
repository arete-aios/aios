# The loop

**The five layers are the parts. The loop is what makes them a system.**

A person with a constitution, tidy folders, twenty skills and a focus file still has a filing cabinet if nothing that goes in ever comes back out and changes a decision. The loop is the only thing that separates an operating system from storage, and it is three steps.

---

## Step 1 · Something happened, and it entered the system

A conversation, a call, a decision, a number, a bad night, a paid invoice, a video worth two minutes of its forty.

**Real mechanisms:** `in7` for the daily line the owner types in their own words. `inbox7` for the folder where files land and get triaged. `voice7` for a voice note transcribed on the owner's own hardware. `meeting7` for a call turned into what was decided and what is owed. `watch7` for a video reduced to a timestamped brief. A calendar and a mailbox are sources feeding this step, not the loop itself.

**How you know it works:** the owner tells you something and does not also have to tell you where to put it.

**Where it breaks:** capture that requires a form. If entering a thought costs more than thinking it, the loop starves at step one and everything downstream looks broken when the real fault is here.

---

## Step 2 · It was written where the next session will see it

Not written somewhere. Written **where the next session reads without being asked**.

**Real mechanisms:** `exit7` at the end of a work session, sweeping everything that exists only in the chat into files, and handing back diffs to approve rather than questions to answer. `context7` keeping one master index per folder, so the AI reads one index instead of a thousand files and nothing becomes invisible by being unlisted. The always loaded core naming the paths that get read every time.

**How you know it works:** close the chat, open a new one tomorrow, and the thing is still there without the owner re-explaining it.

**Where it breaks:** a note filed in a folder no index lists. The AI cannot see what nothing points at, and the owner believes it was saved because they watched it being saved.

---

## Step 3 · It changed what happens next

This is the step people leave out, and this is the step that makes the difference.

**Real mechanisms:** `focus7` rebuilding the one file that says what matters right now, area by area, from real data, with a diff the owner approves. `week7` reading the week's own records once a week, counting the money, naming the patterns, and deciding what changes next week. The daily brief that opens from the focus file rather than from an empty screen. A skill that fires unasked because the constitution told it when to.

**How you know it works:** something written on Monday visibly changed a Thursday. The owner did not go looking for it. It arrived.

**Where it breaks:** everywhere, most of the time. Capture is satisfying, filing feels productive, and both can run for months while nothing ever comes back. The result is a beautifully organised archive of a life that is being lived exactly as it would have been without it.

---

## The test

Ask the owner for one specific instance from the last two weeks:

> **Name one thing your system told you that you had not asked it, and say what you did differently because of it.**

If they can, the loop is closed and the system is working, whatever else is missing. If they cannot, nothing else in the system matters yet, and the correct next action is never to add a skill from outside the loop. It is to name the mechanism for step three and put it on a day of the week.

**One closed loop with three skills beats twenty skills and an open one.** That is not a preference. It is the whole finding.

---

## Where the loop is written down

**In the constitution**, in the owner's own words, with the actual mechanism named at each of the three steps. Not the principle, the mechanism: what captures, what stores, what redirects. A loop described in general terms is a loop nobody can check.

Maintained by: `constitution7` writes it, `week7` turns it, `audit7` checks that all three steps still have something real behind them.
